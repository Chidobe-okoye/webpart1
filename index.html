Our Story

Welcome to Maddison Bakery

We are a family-run artisanal bakery established in 2019 in Cape Town. Everything is baked fresh every morning using quality local ingredients, with warmth and care baked into every loaf, cake, and pastry.

Our goal is to bring joy through homemade goodness in a warm and welcoming environment — your neighbourhood spot for a perfect morning.
