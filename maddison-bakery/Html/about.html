Our Story

Maddison Bakery is a family-run artisanal bakery established in 2019 in Cape Town. We started as a small pop-up stall at local markets and have grown into a beloved neighbourhood café.

Our mission is to create delicious homemade baked goods while building a warm community space for our customers.
